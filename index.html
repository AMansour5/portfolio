Projects

Most are built on my own hardware and machines, so the details come from what I actually ran.

CameraCalibrator

Stereo and monocular calibration

A checkerboard calibrator for a side-by-side USB stereo camera that outputs a 3840×1080 combined frame. It splits the frame into left and right views and calibrates both, and the calibration ran to a successful result.

View the repository

Live disparity tuner

Stereo block matching (SGBM)

A tool for tuning SGBM disparity settings on a live feed. It scales the resolution down so the tuning stays responsive on a 3840×1080 stream.

Depth estimator

In progress, YOLO plus stereo depth

Detects objects on the rectified left frame only, then passes the boxes to a depth module that reports a distance per object. Rectification and disparity live in a helper class, and an orchestrator runs the whole pipeline.

ROV stereo measurement

Robo-Tech, underwater

With a teammate, I estimated a submerged pipe’s length from stereo images to ±0.01 m. I also fixed random camera shutdowns by assigning stable camera names with udev rules, which made capture dependable.

State estimation

EKF, SLAM and Nav2

On my autonomous wheelchair with a robotic arm, an EKF fuses IMU and wheel-encoder data for localization, with lifelong SLAM and the Nav2 planner on top. Separately, I wrote KF, EKF and UKF filters from scratch and compared them on a simulated differential-drive robot.

ROS2 rover lane following

Debugging a vision pipeline

Traced a lane detection to Nav2 pipeline through QoS mismatches, corrupted polynomial fits, unstable navigation goals, watchdog stop frames and a broken topic chain. I added diagnostic instrumentation to the detection and goal nodes so each failure could be found from logs.

Self-driving car in simulation

CARLA, ROS2 Humble, ongoing

A simulated car with four cameras, 3D lidar, encoders and an IMU, driven through the CARLA ROS bridge, with a web operator panel for manual and autonomous modes. It is simulation-only, but built behind hardware-abstraction layers.
